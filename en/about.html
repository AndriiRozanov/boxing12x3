About the project

Boxing 12×3 is an interactive scorecard for evaluating boxing fights. Choose a bout, score it round by round – and get your own result.

It emerged for a simple reason: no convenient equivalent existed. Yet the need to judge fights alongside an audience was there. So the tool appeared, gradually building a boxing guide and pre-fight analysis around it.

What's next – even more content on the biggest bouts, statistics, scoring rules and essential boxing information for audiences around the world.

Data sources

Boxer statistics, records, height, reach and fight dates are compiled from publicly available sources on the internet. Videos are embedded from YouTube and belong to their channel owners; photos belong to their rights holders, with sources credited under each photo. If you notice an inaccuracy or would like any material removed, please contact us.
