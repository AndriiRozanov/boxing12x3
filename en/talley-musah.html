Where to watch Talley vs Musah?

Talley vs Musah is broadcast on Sky Sports (UK and Ireland), Paramount+ (US and Canada) and Setanta Sports (Ukraine). The broadcast starts on December 4 at 12:00 PM ET / 9:00 AM PT / 5:00 PM GMT. The exact start of this fight depends on the running order.

The fight takes place on December 4, 2026 at The Venue (Riyadh Season) in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, as part of Zuffa Boxing 14. The broadcast starts at 12:00 PM ET / 5:00 PM GMT.
