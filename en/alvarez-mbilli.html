Alvarez vs Mbilli: frequently asked questions

Who won Alvarez vs Mbilli?

Saul Alvarez beat Christian Mbilli by unanimous decision (UD 12). The referee was Hector Afu.

What were the scorecards for Alvarez vs Mbilli?

Barry Lindenmann scored it 115-112, Guido Cavalleri 116-111 and Jorge Luciano Gorini 117-110, all for Alvarez.

Was a point deducted in Alvarez vs Mbilli?

Yes. The referee deducted a point from Mbilli in round 8 for a punch to the back of the head.

How did the judges score Alvarez vs Mbilli round by round?

All three judges gave rounds 1, 5, 6 and 7 to Alvarez, and the only round all three gave to Mbilli was round 11. Lindenmann gave Mbilli 5 rounds, Cavalleri 3 and Gorini 3. A point was deducted from Mbilli in round 8. The round-by-round table is in the Scorecards section on this page.

Where and when did Alvarez vs Mbilli take place?

The fight took place on October 31, 2026 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, at the Kingdom Arena, as part of the Mexico vs The World card.

Which title did Alvarez defend against Mbilli?

Saul Alvarez defended the WBC super middleweight title.

How much did Alvarez and Mbilli weigh for the fight?

At the official weigh-in on October 30, 2026, Saul Alvarez weighed 76 kg and Christian Mbilli 76.1 kg. Mbilli was 0.1 kg heavier. Both were within the super middleweight limit of 76.2 kg (168 lbs).

Which other fights are on Mexico vs The World?

The co-main event is Munguia vs Zon. The card also includes: Espinoza vs Blandon, Picasso vs Martin, Tsutsumi vs Rojas Garcia, Rojo vs Biea and more. The full card is on the Mexico vs The World page.
