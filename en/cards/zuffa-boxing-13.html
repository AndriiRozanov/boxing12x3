Zuffa Boxing 13: frequently asked questions

Who won the main event at Zuffa Boxing 13?

Carlos Adames beat Jerome Warburton by unanimous decision (100-90, 98-92, 99-91) over 10 rounds at a 165 lb catchweight.

When did Zuffa Boxing 13 take place?

November 14, 2026 at The Venue (Riyadh Season) in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

Where to watch Zuffa Boxing events?

Zuffa Boxing events are broadcast on Sky Sports (UK and Ireland), Paramount+ (US and Canada) and Setanta Sports (Ukraine).

Which fights were on Zuffa Boxing 13?

The main event was Adames vs Warburton and the co-main event was Moloney vs Teran. The card also included Surace vs Bednarek, Castro vs Butaev, Kuchmenov vs Ortiz, Boymatov vs Kanla, Zokirov vs Colantonio, Awaji vs Lubega and Alotebi vs Ssali.

Which fights ended early at Zuffa Boxing 13?

Boymatov knocked out Kanla in round 1, Zokirov beat Colantonio by corner retirement (RTD) in round 1 and Awaji stopped Lubega in round 3.
