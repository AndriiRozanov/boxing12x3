Mexico vs The World: frequently asked questions

Where to watch Mexico vs The World?

Mexico vs The World is broadcast on DAZN. The broadcast starts on October 31 at 10:00 AM ET / 7:00 AM PT / 2:00 PM GMT; the main card starts at 12:00 PM ET / 9:00 AM PT / 4:00 PM GMT.

When is Mexico vs The World?

October 31, 2026 at the Kingdom Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The event is organized by Riyadh Season.

Who is in the main event of Mexico vs The World?

In the main event Saul Alvarez defends the WBC super middleweight title against Christian Mbilli. In the co-main event Jaime Munguia faces Nikita Zon.

Which fights are on Mexico vs The World?

The main event is Alvarez vs Mbilli and the co-main event is Munguia vs Zon. The card also includes: Espinoza vs Blandon, Picasso vs Martin, Tsutsumi vs Rojas Garcia, Rojo vs Biea and more.
