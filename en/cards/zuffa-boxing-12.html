Main event: Billam-Smith vs Clarke

The Zuffa 12 matchup between Chris Billam-Smith and Cheavon Clarke is the next step for the British ex-champion toward a potential title in the promotion. Since June 2026, he has been boxing under the Zuffa banner and already has a successful debut in the promotion under his belt.

He faced Ryan Rozicki, putting on a wild, action-packed show over seven rounds. The opponents completely forgot about defense, focusing on inflicting as much pain and damage on each other as possible. Billam-Smith entered the ring as the favorite and lived up to that status. His skills proved superior even in non-stop exchanges, and the fight was stopped in the 7th round.

Clarke arrives to face the former world champion on a two-fight stoppage streak. In 2025, he lost to Viddal Riley by decision, then knocked out Anthony Holloway first, followed by the tough Jack Massey.

Cheavon is unlikely to be a walk in the park for Billam-Smith. He has a higher knockout percentage, a solid record, and no stoppage losses in his career. Breaking down an opponent like this is an extra task for Chris, and forcing a purely technical boxing match is unlikely, given the fighting styles of both men.

It shouldn't come as a surprise if the Brits put on a fierce battle, filled with exchanges, active clinching, and heavy punches from both sides.
