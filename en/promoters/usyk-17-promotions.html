Usyk 17 Promotions: frequently asked questions

When is the next Usyk 17 Promotions event?

The next event is Rising Stars III: Constellation on December 19, 2026 in Lviv. The main event is Oleksandr Khyzhnyak against an opponent who has not been announced yet. Details and the full card will be announced later.

Where to watch Usyk 17 Promotions?

Rising Stars and Rising Stars II were shown on DAZN (Europe and USA) and Kyivstar TV (Ukraine). The first two shows, Big Boxing Night and Ultimate Boxing Night, were on XSport.

Where do Usyk 17 Promotions events take place?

Equides Club in Lisnyky (Kyiv Oblast) hosted two events, WePlay Esports Arena in Kyiv one and !FESTrepublic in Lviv one. The next event is planned in Lviv.

How many Usyk 17 Promotions fights ended in a stoppage?

Across the first 4 events, 20 of 42 fights ended by stoppage (KO, TKO, RTD), or 48%. The average stoppage round is 2.15.

Which titles were on the line at Usyk 17 Promotions events?

At Rising Stars, Daniel Lapin defended the WBA Continental, IBF Intercontinental and WBO International titles against Kristaps Bulmeistars. At Rising Stars II, Oleksandr Khyzhnyak won the vacant WBA Gold International title against Durval Elias Palacio.
